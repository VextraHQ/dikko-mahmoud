# Website scope addendum

**Status: Draft awaiting client acknowledgement**

Prepared for Dikko & Mahmoud by Vextra Limited on 31 October 2026, with reference
to the revised Website Redesign and Development proposal dated 16 September 2026.

To align the website with the consolidated design feedback, Vextra proposes the
following inclusions within the existing project fee:

- Increase the practice-area content migration allowance from eight to **nine
  practice areas**, using the shared dynamic practice-detail presentation.
- Include a standalone **SME retainer page**, presenting the Advisory and
  Secretarial tracks using content supplied or approved by the firm.
- Include a **Terms of Use page**, alongside the privacy notice already included,
  using text supplied or reviewed and approved by the firm.

The total project fee remains **₦1,400,000**. No additional fee is proposed for
these inclusions. All other migration allowances, payment arrangements and
provisions of the original proposal remain unchanged.

The firm remains responsible for confirming the final practice names and
grouping, current biographies and qualifications, approved SME and legal-page
content, contact information and permission to identify each client or matter.
Items awaiting publication consent will remain hidden from the website.

The remaining delivery schedule will be coordinated around approved designs and
receipt of required inputs. This draft does not establish a revised launch date
or record client acceptance. The original proposal is preserved unchanged.

Please confirm acknowledgement of these scope inclusions through the designated
project contact.
